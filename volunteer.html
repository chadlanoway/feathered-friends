Life at Feathered Friends

See our volunteers in action

Volunteers are the lifeblood of any nonprofit organization, but they are especially important to us and our birds.

Whether you're interested in in-shelter bird care, education and outreach, or helping plan special events, we would love to hear from you. We need you!

In-Shelter Care of Birds

This includes cleaning cages, preparing food, bathing, feeding, watering, and socializing the birds. You can help make the birds' stay more enjoyable and prepare them for their new homes.

Humane Education

One way to reduce the overpopulation of captive birds is through education. Volunteers visit classrooms with Feathered Friends staff members to teach children about responsible animal care and pet ownership.

Adoption Outreach

Feathered Friends occasionally has birds available for adoption at off-site locations. Outreach volunteers educate the public about our sanctuary, explain our policies and procedures, and answer questions about available birds.

Special Events

Feathered Friends attends special events and fundraisers throughout the year. Volunteers may help set up our area, staff an information booth, answer questions about the sanctuary, and assist with many other activities.

Other Skills

We welcome people offering services such as skilled trades, handyman work, landscaping, veterinary care, accounting, photography, marketing, or the many other skills needed to operate a rescue. If you think you have something to offer, please contact us.

Fostering

Many people contact us with an interest in fostering. We do not currently have a fostering program except in rare circumstances when a bird requires highly specialized care. Thank you for your interest.
