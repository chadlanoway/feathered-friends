Parrot boarding

Review our health requirements and rates, then submit one boarding request for each bird.

Before you apply

Health requirements

We require proof of a veterinary exam performed by an avian veterinarian before a bird can board at our facility. Any diagnostics recommended by your veterinarian should be completed.

Birds must be examined or tested for these communicable diseases before boarding:

  • Psittacosis
  • Psittacine Beak and Feather Disease (PBFD)
  • Avian Polyomavirus (APV)
  • Psittacine Herpes Virus (PsHV-1)
  • Avian Bornavirus (ABV)

The completed boarding contract must be received at least one week before the requested stay.

Active, consistent volunteers may qualify for boarding discounts after volunteering for 90 days at a minimum of two shifts per month.

Food and scheduling

Planning your bird's stay

We provide fresh chop or mash in the morning and TOP's pellets in the evening at no additional cost. If your bird eats another diet, please provide its usual food to avoid an abrupt transition. We will not serve food that is unsafe for parrots and may use our food at our discretion if unsuitable food is supplied.

Requested drop-off and pickup times depend on volunteer availability and are not confirmed until a Feathered Friends representative contacts you.
